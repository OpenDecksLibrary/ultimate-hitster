Frequently asked questions about Hitster Guilty Pleasures

How many cards are in Hitster Guilty Pleasures?

Hitster Guilty Pleasures (France) contains 308 cards. Every card has a QR code on one side and the artist, title and release year on the other. The table above lists all 308 of them in card order, so you can look up any card from the box.

Which years does Hitster Guilty Pleasures cover?

The songs in Hitster Guilty Pleasures run from 1953 to 2024. The 2000s are the best represented decade, with 63 of the 308 cards we have a release year for. The year column in the table above gives the exact year for every card.

Is Hitster Guilty Pleasures a standalone game or an expansion?

Hitster Guilty Pleasures is a standalone game, not an expansion. The box holds all 308 cards, so you do not need another set to play. What you add yourself is a phone to scan the QR codes and a music app to play the songs.

Which artists appear most often in Hitster Guilty Pleasures?

The artists with the most cards in Hitster Guilty Pleasures are France Gall (2 cards), Irene Cara (2 cards), Jean-Jacques Goldman (2 cards), Jeanne Mas (2 cards) and Joe Dassin (2 cards). Across the 308 cards we have song data for, 295 different artists appear, and 13 of them have more than one card in the box.

Can I mix Hitster Guilty Pleasures with my other editions?

If you mix editions, nothing breaks: each edition has its own QR codes pointing at its own songs, so a mixed deck still scans to the right track.

How do I find the year of a card in Hitster Guilty Pleasures?

Look up the card number in the table above: it gives the artist, title and release year for every card we have song data for, 308 of them in this edition. Hitify checks release years against MusicBrainz and Discogs, so if a year still looks wrong you can suggest a correction from the card page.

Can I make my own music cards?

Yes. Hitify turns any public Spotify, YouTube, Tidal, Deezer or Apple Music playlist into printable music cards, each with a QR code that plays the song. The first 100 cards are free.
