Frequently asked questions about Hitster Original Refresh

How many cards are in Hitster Original Refresh?

Hitster Original Refresh (France) contains 308 cards. Every card has a QR code on one side and the artist, title and release year on the other. The table above lists all 308 of them in card order, so you can look up any card from the box.

Which years does Hitster Original Refresh cover?

The songs in Hitster Original Refresh run from 1908 to 2025. The 2010s are the best represented decade, with 53 of the 308 cards we have a release year for. The year column in the table above gives the exact year for every card.

Is Hitster Original Refresh a standalone game or an expansion?

Hitster Original Refresh is a standalone game, not an expansion. The box holds all 308 cards, so you do not need another set to play. What you add yourself is a phone to scan the QR codes and a music app to play the songs.

Which artists appear most often in Hitster Original Refresh?

The artists with the most cards in Hitster Original Refresh are Alicia Keys (2 cards), Billie Eilish (2 cards), Elvis Presley (2 cards), France Gall (2 cards) and Harry Styles (2 cards). Across the 308 cards we have song data for, 297 different artists appear, and 11 of them have more than one card in the box.

Can I mix Hitster Original Refresh with my other editions?

If you mix editions, nothing breaks: each edition has its own QR codes pointing at its own songs, so a mixed deck still scans to the right track.

How do I find the year of a card in Hitster Original Refresh?

Look up the card number in the table above: it gives the artist, title and release year for every card we have song data for, 308 of them in this edition. Hitify checks release years against MusicBrainz and Discogs, so if a year still looks wrong you can suggest a correction from the card page.

Can I make my own music cards?

Yes. Hitify turns any public Spotify, YouTube, Tidal, Deezer or Apple Music playlist into printable music cards, each with a QR code that plays the song. The first 100 cards are free.
