# Ultimate Hitster v0.3.0-beta.1 — candidate notes

**Unpublished candidate.** PR review, merge and manual validation are still
required. These notes do not announce an existing release.

## Features

- Discover available Hitify editions by market, language, family and revision.
- Import the complete Original Refresh France and Guilty Pleasures France editions
  in addition to the existing Original France dataset.
- Generate online/offline packages for an edition, country, family, explicit
  selection or the complete imported catalogue.
- Share recording identities and study cards across editions where the recording
  and catalogue year agree. Keep genuinely distinct IDs/versions and year variants.
- Add hierarchical country, language, family, edition, card and decade tags.
- Retain the minimal audio-only front and artist/title/year/optional album answer.

Statistics, counts, source conflicts and missing references/previews are listed
in data/reports/catalogue.json and the candidate validation.json. The latter
records the exact build commit, package counts and checksums. Uncertain references
are not silently replaced. Physical recording/version verification remains open.

## Update without losing progress

Back up the collection, then import into the existing collection without deleting
its production notes/decks. Original France keeps the published v1 identities.
For an equal/older-timestamp alternative, select Update existing notes: Always.
Keep existing card placement and scheduling. Online and offline are alternatives
for the same notes; they do not create two sets of progress.

Shared cards stay in their current deck when another edition or a wider selection
is imported. Newly introduced cards go into Ultimate Hitster; the legacy Original
France package keeps its existing deck. Use edition tags/filtered decks for
overlapping collections, or move cards in Browse without deleting them.
Importing a smaller selection does not remove previously imported songs.
Prototype note types and progress remain separate.

## Known limitations

- Online playback requires Internet and Spotify's external MP3 preview service.
  URLs can expire/change or be unavailable by territory; refresh pinned references,
  recheck audio and rebuild. No full-song stream or replacement is inferred.
- Offline packages include checked excerpts and are larger. Media require a
  complete sync before mobile offline testing.
- Hitify catalogue years can differ between editions or from provider/reissue
  dates. Variant years remain separate study facts, with the same recording/media.
- Different Spotify IDs are not automatically merged by title. Potential aliases
  require evidence and review; distinct versions remain distinct.
- Languages classify the edition market; they are not per-song language claims.
- Source/provider conflicts and absent previews are retained and reported.
  Validation builds can include a player without audio; strict builds reject them.
- Automated Anki-engine tests do not establish actual GUI/device playback.
  Desktop full-edition, AnkiDroid and AnkiMobile results remain pending.
- The available registry is wider than the imported catalogue. No other editions
  are implicitly downloaded by a country/global build.

No stable release or full-world import is included.
